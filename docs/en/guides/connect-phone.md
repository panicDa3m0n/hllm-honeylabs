# Connect your phone

1. Open the HLLM app and tap **Add machine**.
2. Your machine shows a **6-digit code** that changes every few seconds; the app shows the same code. Confirm that they match.
3. From now on every connection is end-to-end encrypted and protected by rotating codes.
4. Turn on **ephemeral screens** to receive requests from your agents and notices when a job is done.
