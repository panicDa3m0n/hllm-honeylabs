# Your smart home

1. In the app open **Home → Add devices**: HLLM OS finds compatible devices on your network.
2. Give rooms and devices a name, or just tell HLLM: "the lamp by the sofa is the reading light".
3. Ask in plain words: "dim the reading light at 22:00 every evening".
