# Choose a personality (Unlocked)

Available on machines you own.

1. In the app open **Mind → Personality**.
2. Describe who you want: "a strict but fair coach who calls me by my surname".
3. Set the **tone** and the **rules**: topics, bluntness, language.
4. Try it, adjust it, save it. You can keep several personalities and switch between them.
