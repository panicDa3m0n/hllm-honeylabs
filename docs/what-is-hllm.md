# What is HLLM

## First: what is an AI model?

An AI model is a program that has read an enormous amount of text and learned how language and ideas connect. You write to it in plain words, it answers in plain words: it can explain, summarize, write, plan and reason.

Most AI assistants today live on big company servers. You send your words over the internet, they answer, and when the chat is over they mostly forget you.

## HLLM: Human LLM

**HLLM** stands for *Human LLM*: a language model with a human-like way of thinking. We designed it around four ideas:

1. **It remembers like a person.** It keeps the present in focus and finds the past when it matters: a document from months ago, something you said last year.
2. **It knows what it doesn't know.** Instead of inventing, it says "I'm not sure" and goes to check.
3. **It sleeps.** At night it reorganizes the day: hundreds of moments become a few clear notes. Tomorrow it knows you a little better.
4. **It lives with you.** It runs on a machine in your home. Your words never travel to someone else's server.

## An example

> **You (in March):** My sister's birthday is the 14th of June, she loves Japanese gardens.
>
> **HLLM (in June, on its own):** Your sister's birthday is in 5 days. There's a bonsai workshop near her on Saturday: want me to check the price?
