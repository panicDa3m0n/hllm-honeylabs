# FAQ

**Do I need to know anything about AI?**
No. You talk to HLLM the way you talk to a person.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes. Only web research and remote access from your phone need a connection.

**Is it the cloud?**
No. HLLM runs on a machine in your home, or on a dedicated machine that only you use.

**Do my conversations train the model?**
Never.

**What is Unlocked?**
A version of the model where you decide personality and rules. Only on machines you own. → [Models](models.md)

**Do I pay a monthly fee?**
Not if you buy the machine. Subscription is an option for those who prefer it.

**How long is my machine supported?**
5 years of security updates.

**Can I contribute?**
Yes: share ideas in [Discussions](../../../discussions).
